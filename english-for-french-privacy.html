French to English Flashcards Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

French to English Flashcards is a English vocabulary and phrase practice app for French speakers. The app is designed to work without an account and with learning progress stored locally.

Information We Collect

We do not operate user accounts or receive your learning progress. Google AdMob and its consent tools may process device identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostic data and approximate location derived from IP addresses to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, subject to your consent choices. Apple processes purchases.

Local App Data

The app may save learning progress, card status, and related practice settings locally on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Accounts, Ads, and Tracking

  • No account is required.
  • The free version includes Google AdMob advertising. The full-version purchase removes advertising.
  • Consent and privacy choices are provided through Google’s consent tools where applicable. You may change available privacy choices in Ad Options.
  • We do not request precise device location. Ad providers may infer approximate location from your IP address.

Children

The app is for general learning and is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
