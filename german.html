German Flashcards Quest

English-to-German vocabulary, phrases and noun-article practice for iPhone and iPad.

Support

For questions, bug reports or support, email mattdeboth@hotmail.com. Include the app name, iOS version and a description of the issue. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Using the app

The app includes 160 vocabulary cards, 80 practical phrases and 100 noun-article/plural exercises. No sign-in or payment is needed. Use Reveal German, then Got it or Review again. Your Progress tab contains missed-card review and a reset option.

Pronunciation uses German voices available on your device. If audio is unavailable, install a German voice in the device's Spoken Content accessibility settings. The learning content remains available without audio.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. This policy applies specifically to German Flashcards Quest (com.flashquest.german), developed by Matt DeBoth.

We do not collect or transmit personal information through this version of the app. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, accounts, in-app purchases or cross-app tracking.

Learning progress, missed cards, practice counts and streak dates are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to the developer or synchronized to a developer service. Clear them using Reset learning progress, or remove the app. Your own device backups may retain app data under your device backup settings.

German speech is generated using available system speech voices. The app does not access a microphone, record audio, request photos, or send study answers to a developer server.

If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Parents may contact us about privacy questions. If app features or data practices change, this policy and App Store disclosures will be updated.
