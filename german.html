German Flashcards Quest

English-to-German vocabulary, phrases and noun-article practice for iPhone and iPad.

Support

Email mattdeboth@hotmail.com with the app name, iOS version and issue. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Free sections and full collection

Only Greetings & Basics and Numbers 1–20 are free: 40 cards. All other vocabulary topics, all phrases and Article Lab require the one-time Full Collection purchase. No subscription. The app contains 180 vocabulary cards, 80 practical phrases and 100 noun-article exercises.

Tap Unlock everything on the collection offer to see your App Store price. Restore purchases with the same Apple Account from Restore purchases. Existing full-collection purchasers retain access. Refunds or revoked purchases remove paid access. No account with the developer is needed.

Use Reveal German, then Got it or Review again. Your Progress tab contains accessible missed-card review and a reset option. Pronunciation uses installed German system voices; learning cards work without audio.

Privacy Policy

Updated October 19, 2026. This policy applies to German Flashcards Quest (com.flashquest.german), developed by Matt DeBoth.

We do not collect or transmit personal information through the app. No advertising, third-party analytics, developer accounts or cross-app tracking.
