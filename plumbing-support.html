Common Notes

  • No sign-in is required.
  • The app includes free starter cards and an optional full unlock.
  • Progress is stored locally on your device.
  • Pipe Math Bench is free. Enter assumed run/fall or constant-flow inputs to practice arithmetic; it is not an installation or code-compliance tool.
  • The optional Full Collection Unlock and Remove Ads purchases are separate one-time products. Use Restore Purchase in the full-unlock screen or Restore Purchases in Ad-Free Options to restore the corresponding purchase.
  • Ad-supported versions use Google AdMob with non-personalized ad requests. See the privacy policy for details.
