English Flashcards Quest

English learning for Spanish speakers, with flashcards, phrases and Conversation Studio sentence-building exercises. Conversation Studio and the starter cards are free. An optional one-time Full Collection unlock provides the remaining card content. Remove Ads is a separate one-time option; a verified full-collection purchase also grants ad-free access. Open the purchase screen and choose Restaurar compra to restore collection access, or use the bottom ad-options bar and Restore Purchases for ad removal.

PPL Test Prep Flashcards

Independent ground-school study with flashcards, practice tests and the Wind Lab component demonstration. It is not an official exam or an operational flight tool. Optional one-time Remove Ads is available from the bottom toolbar. Use Restore Purchases in Ad-Free Options to restore ad removal. Use approved aircraft documents and instruction for actual flight decisions.

Paralegal Prep Flashcards

Independent study with flashcards, practice tests and Case Analysis Lab fictional reasoning exercises. It is not legal advice or an official certification exam. Keep real client or confidential information out of the local study notebook. Optional one-time Remove Ads and Restore Purchases are in Ad-Free Options, opened from the bottom toolbar.

Privacy and local data

These versions include Google AdMob advertising and Google UMP consent controls. Requests are non-personalized, but the SDK may process approximate location, device or installation identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic information. Study answers, bookmarks and notebook text remain on your device and are not sent to Google for advertising. No developer account or sign-in is required.

Read the current advertising privacy policy for data practices and retention. Open Ad-Free Options → Ad Privacy Choices to review consent choices where available. Apple processes purchases; we do not receive payment-card details. Restore using the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Remove local study data through any in-app reset or by deleting the app, subject to device backups.
